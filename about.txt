# BeirutPoly

**BeirutPoly** is a creative twist on the classic Monopoly game, inspired by the vibrant streets, neighborhoods, and landmarks of Beirut. From **Hamra** to **Downtown**, **Gemmayzeh** to **Verdun**, players buy, trade, and build on the city's most iconic areas.

Instead of traditional Chance and Community Chest cards, experience real-life Beirut moments — like sudden traffic, café gossip, or a surprise street protest!

Whether you're collecting rent on **Zaitunay Bay** or stuck in traffic at **Cola**, *BeirutPoly* is your chance to own the city, one street at a time.
